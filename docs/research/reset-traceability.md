## Detailed property dispositions

`KEEP` preserves the property under a smaller owner. `CHANGE` preserves its safety purpose with an explicit new mechanism or scope.
`HISTORY` preserves executable meaning and evidence limits in status or source-owned fixtures. `DROP` removes a proposed requirement for the stated reason.
Mixed parent sections retain their core properties below. A parent section's disposition cannot override an individual property row.
Destination headings identify current contract owners, not proposed new documents.
