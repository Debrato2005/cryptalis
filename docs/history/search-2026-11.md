## Internal exact text prefix prototype: 2026-11-01

query-prefix bounds. [Compatibility](../compatibility.md#internal-exact-prefix-prototype),
[manifest](../manifest.md#internal-prefix-engineering-syntax),
[security](../security.md#internal-packed-exact-prefix-prototype) and
[lifecycle](../lifecycle.md#internal-exact-text-prefix-lifecycle) own its contracts.

This is **INTERNAL ONLY**, not admitted support. Ordinary planning, previous
locks, saved preparation/activation artifacts, attachment and retained runtime
or field-aware crypto operations refuse outside a private synthetic fixture
scope. A disabled-prefix successor still retains its historical source-lock
obligation. Doctor reports unadmitted capability before inspection; under the
fixture scope it can admit sample structure while its overall diagnosis remains

not satisfy the unchanged admission gates or authorize real data.

startswith escapes, Unicode code points without normalization, NULL/empty

pagination. Sync/async writes retain current values, RETURNING and savepoints.

payloads refuse. Custom and generic native plans can use the GIN expression

control, not a million-row plan or latency qualification.
